# ONDA Homepage Redesign — Multi-Script UI/UX Design System Specification

> **Document Name:** `ONDA-Homepage-Design-System.md`  
> **Brand Identity:** ONDA (Office National des Droits d'Auteur et Droits Voisins / الديوان الوطني لحقوق المؤلف والحقوق المجاورة)  
> **Languages & Scripts Supported:** Arabic (RTL), French (LTR), English (LTR), Tamazight / Tifinagh  
> **Target Framework:** Modern Web (Tailwind CSS, Responsive, Dark/Light Mode)

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Logo Color Extraction

The visual language is extracted directly from the official ONDA seal:

* **Primary Brand Blue (ONDA Royal Blue):** `#1B669D` | `rgb(27, 102, 157)` | `hsl(205, 71%, 36%)`
  * *Symbolism:* Institutional trust, copyright protection, legal governance, and security.
* **Secondary Brand Green (ONDA Emerald Teal):** `#1C9976` | `rgb(28, 153, 118)` | `hsl(163, 69%, 35%)`
  * *Symbolism:* Creative vitality, artist empowerment, culture, and digital modernization.
* **Brand Geometry:** Dynamic circular quadrants and 4-petal seal used as subtle background watermarks, status icons, and modern glass card accents.

### 4.2 Multi-Language Navbar Layout
* **Top Bar:** 
  * Left (RTL Right): Support hotline & urgent author announcements.
  * Right (RTL Left): Language switcher toggle pills `[عربي | Français | English | ⵜⴰⵎⴰⵣⵉⵖⵜ]`.
* **Main Header:**
  * **Brand Zone:** Official ONDA Logo (Vector SVG).
  * **Nav Zone:** Responsive menu (`Accueil`, `Services en ligne`, `Répertoire`, `Réglementation`, `Contact`).
  * **Controls:** Theme Toggle (`Light/Dark`), Search modal trigger (`Ctrl+K`), and Primary Teal Button **"E-Portail"**.
